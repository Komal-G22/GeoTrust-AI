## 📌 About

GeoTrust AI is an AI-powered framework that evaluates the **reliability, consistency, and confidence** of geospatial information collected from multiple data sources.

Instead of simply visualizing geospatial data, GeoTrust AI analyzes multiple sources, detects inconsistencies, generates an AI-based **Trust Score**, and explains why a particular insight should (or should not) be trusted.

This project is being developed for the **Emerging Technologies Hackathon 2026**.

---

## 🌟 Vision

GeoTrust AI aims to become a trusted AI framework that enables governments, researchers, emergency response teams, and organizations to make confident decisions using reliable geospatial intelligence.

By combining Artificial Intelligence with Explainable AI (XAI), the framework promotes transparency, trust, and accountability in geospatial analytics.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Geospatial intelligence is generated from multiple sources such as satellite imagery, weather data, GIS layers, IoT sensors, and government reports.

These sources often provide **conflicting, incomplete, or inconsistent information**, making it difficult for decision-makers to determine which data can be trusted.

Existing platforms primarily focus on visualizing geospatial information but do not evaluate its **reliability, consistency, or confidence**.

This can lead to inaccurate decisions in disaster management, agriculture, environmental monitoring, urban planning, and other critical domains.

GeoTrust AI addresses this challenge by introducing an AI-powered Trust Engine that evaluates the reliability of geospatial insights and provides transparent explanations for every decision.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The primary objectives of GeoTrust AI are:

- Evaluate the reliability of geospatial information from multiple sources.
- Detect inconsistencies and conflicts across datasets.
- Generate an AI-powered Trust Score for every geospatial insight.
- Provide Explainable AI (XAI) to justify trust decisions.
- Enable faster and more informed decision-making.
- Build a scalable framework that can integrate additional geospatial data sources in the future.
